Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how MothLight currently handles information.

1. Introduction

MothLight is a mobile game published by PureNest. This Privacy Policy explains what information the current version of MothLight does and does not handle. It applies to the MothLight iOS app.

2. Information We Collect

MothLight does not collect personal information. The current version does not transmit user data off the device and does not send information to PureNest or to any other party.

MothLight does not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers. It does not have user accounts. It does not access the camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, or Bluetooth.

3. Information Stored Locally

MothLight may store some information locally on your device so the game can remember your preferences and progress. This information stays on your device and is used to provide the gameplay experience.

Local information may include:

  • Game settings
  • Music preferences
  • Sound effect preferences
  • Haptic preferences
  • Theme preferences
  • Selected lantern
  • Selected game mode
  • Selected journey distance
  • Local playtime information
  • Local game scores
  • Local run history
  • Journey completion progress
  • Local leaderboard data

The leaderboard is currently local only. There is no online leaderboard, account system, cloud synchronization, or server backend.

4. Analytics and Tracking

MothLight does not currently use analytics, advertising tracking, or cross-app tracking.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of MothLight does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or backend services that collect user data.

6. Children's Privacy

MothLight does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

7. Data Security

Because gameplay information is currently stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to MothLight servers, PureNest does not currently maintain a server containing user gameplay or personal data for MothLight.

No method of storage is completely secure. You can help protect locally stored progress by keeping your device secure and by using your device's built-in protections.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if MothLight's functionality changes. When we update it, we will change the Last Updated date on this page. Please review this page from time to time.
